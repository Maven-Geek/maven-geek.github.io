What I learned building a facial recognition attendance system

1 Nov 2026, 6 min read

During my SIWES internship at the Obafemi Awolowo University Computer Centre, I built a desktop app that takes attendance by recognising faces from a live camera feed. [Add one or two sentences on why attendance needed automating and who would use it.]

How it works

The app is written in Python. OpenCV reads frames from the camera, the face_recognition library turns each face into a numeric encoding, and the app compares those encodings against the faces it has already registered. When it finds a match, it writes the person's name and the time to an Excel sheet with openpyxl. The interface is built with CustomTkinter.
